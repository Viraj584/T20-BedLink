Color palette

Health tech needs to feel calm and trustworthy, but this app also has to communicate urgency. So the brand color is a calm clinical teal, and the strong colors (green/amber/red) are reserved for status only. Red never decorates anything, so when a nurse or paramedic sees it, it means something.

Role	Name	Hex
Primary	Clinical Teal	#0E7C86
Primary dark (headers, pressed)	Deep Teal	#0A5560
Primary tint (chips, highlights)	Mist Teal	#E0F2F4
Background	Cool White	#F5F9FA
Surface (cards)	White	#FFFFFF
Text primary	Ink	#12262B
Text secondary	Slate	#55696E
Borders/dividers	Fog	#D5E2E5
Available / fresh / accepted	Go Green	#1E9E5A (tint #E3F5EB)
Warning / aging data / medium load	Caution Amber	#E8A317 (tint #FFF4D9, text on tint #7A5200)
Full / stale / reject / critical	Alert Red	#D32F2F (tint #FDE7E7)
Info / links	Info Blue	#2B7BD6
